## FX Unleashed 0.6.0

Updates itself from 0.5.x: the banner (or the About tab) offers it, one click installs it, **Roll back** is there if you want the old one.

### What's new

- **The Mustang GT3 dash, rebuilt 1:1.** The LMGT3 Ford Mustang GT3 (converted from
  [Redadeg's SimHub dash on lmu-dashboards.com](https://lmu-dashboards.com)) now has everything the original has: both pages above the
  settings row (tyres and brakes, and the delta bar with lap time and position), every overlay (setting changes, lap summaries per session,
  the pit limiter and pit stop screens, flags, low energy, engine off, pit request, lift and coast), the ignition-on sweep and the
  ignition-off screen, and the headlights dimming the screen. Checked item by item against the original. The speed above the oval is in a
  bigger font, the oval sits a little lower, and the wiper icon only shows with the wipers on (LMU reports 1 with them off).
- **Dash pages.** A dash can have pages (`Pages`, and `page:N` in an element's conditions). Flip them with **Next page / Previous page**:
  SimHub actions you can bind to any button, wheel buttons, or the buttons next to **Demo on the wheel** on the Dashes tab. The designer
  has a pages editor. Dashes with pages use dash format 3 and need this version.
- **Ovals and rounded boxes drawn by the screen itself.** The FX Pro's screen can draw smooth (anti-aliased) shapes of its own: the
  plugin now draws overlays' ovals, rings and rounded boxes that way instead of keeping a picture of each in the screen's memory. They are
  smooth with or without the screen's RAM patch, in any colour, and the Mustang needs about half the screen memory it did (77 KB, was
  158 KB). A setting pop-up going away now puts back only what was under it, not a block of the dash around it.
- **Dim.** A dash element that darkens the whole screen while its conditions hold (the Mustang's headlights), with the backlight: nothing
  is redrawn.
- **Checks for dash makers.** `fxdash verify --overlays` brings up every overlay on every page and reports what each costs and whether
  anything flashes; `--demo` runs exactly what **Demo on the wheel** shows. The designer has an overlay picker and the same check, and
  **Make them take turns** for values half under an overlay.
- **SimHub import.** Pages, overlay screens, blinking items, layer opacity, leaderboard items and the speed in the user's unit come over.
- **Slow updates in the log.** A dash update that sends over 6 KB or takes over half a second is written to SimHub's log with what it drew.

Everything else is as in [0.5.3](https://github.com/fxunleashed/fx-unleashed/releases/tag/v0.5.3).

### Requirements

SimHub 9.11 or newer, SimPro Manager 3 (V3.2.2 tested) for standard mode, an FX Pro or GT Neo. Tested by the maintainer on one FX Pro on an Alpha EVO
base: other bases, wheels and games may behave differently, so please report what you find (Discord or GitHub issues).

### Install and update

From 0.5.x: let it update itself (About tab). New install: close SimHub, copy `User.FXProRpmSync.dll` from the zip into your SimHub folder, start SimHub,
turn the plugin on. Guide: https://fxunleashed.com/start/
