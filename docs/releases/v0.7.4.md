## FX Unleashed 0.7.4

Updates itself from 0.5.x, 0.6.x and 0.7.x: the banner (or the About tab) offers it, one click installs it, **Roll back** is there if you want the old one.

### What's new

**Lights**

- **Preview a state on the wheel.** On the Lights tab, the "Preview as" buttons (Driving, Pit limiter, Engine start, No game, In a
  menu, Engine off, Engine stop) now play on the real wheel too, not just on the picture. A state plays once and deselects by
  itself: the start-up and shutdown run through once, the steady states show for five seconds. Click the one that's playing to stop
  it. It works with no game running, in a menu or standing still; while you're driving the wheel keeps showing the real thing.
- **"Driving" is a whole lap.** It runs one lap of the demo car from a standing start: up through the gears, braking, ABS and TC,
  a flag. Changing a setting that belongs to a state (start-up, shutdown, the limiter lights) plays that state again.
- **Twinkling presets (Neon Tokyo, Hyperspace, Abyss):** a light between two stars is now off. It used to keep a faint glow that
  looked dark on the page but showed on the wheel as lights that wouldn't go out. While driving with "Hold still", every light is
  lit steadily in the star colours, instead of a frozen moment with most lights dim and one or two lit.

**GT Neo**

- **Rings and buttons change together.** A change to many lights at once (the four rings flashing for a flag, a start-up, a state
  change) used to reach the wheel in two steps a frame apart, so part of a ring switched late. The buttons and rings now go out
  together; the rev lights still update every frame.

Everything else is as in [0.7.3](https://github.com/fxunleashed/fx-unleashed/releases/tag/v0.7.3).

### Requirements

SimHub 9.11 or newer, SimPro Manager 3 (V3.2.2 tested) for standard mode, an FX Pro, GT Neo or FX. This release was tried by the
maintainer on a GT Neo on an Alpha EVO base; on the FX Pro and the FX the same changes are checked offline. Please report what you
find (Discord or GitHub issues).

### Install and update

From 0.5.x, 0.6.x or 0.7.x: let it update itself (About tab). New install: close SimHub, copy `User.FXProRpmSync.dll` from the zip into your SimHub folder, start SimHub,
turn the plugin on. Guide: https://fxunleashed.com/start/
