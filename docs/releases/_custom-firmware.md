### Custom firmware for Unleashed mode

Unleashed mode (your own dashes, all 38 lights, the dash button) needs our custom firmware for the wheel, build {BUILD} or newer. Standard mode needs nothing.

**The whole path in one guide: [fxunleashed.com/start](https://fxunleashed.com/start/)** (about 15 minutes, every step says what you should see)

1. [Custom firmware](https://fxunleashed.com/start/#1-install-the-custom-firmware): **[download it here](https://github.com/fxunleashed/fx-unleashed-firmware/releases/latest)** and flash it with SimPro (Settings > Update > Manual Firmware Flash). Please read the **[short notice](https://fxunleashed.com/firmware/#risks)** first: it is your wheel's own program, at your own risk, and Simagic's original is always one flash away.
2. [This plugin](https://fxunleashed.com/start/#2-install-the-plugin) (the zip below).
3. [The screen's RAM patch](https://fxunleashed.com/start/#3-turn-on-picture-memory) (picture memory): optional, but highly recommended.
4. [Your dashes and lights](https://fxunleashed.com/start/#4-pick-your-dashes-and-lights).
