## FX Unleashed 0.7.6

Updates itself from 0.5.x, 0.6.x and 0.7.x: the banner (or the About tab) offers it, one click installs it, **Roll back** is there if you want the old one.

### What's new

- **iRacing cars' own shift lights.** 131 iRacing cars now light the wheel the way their dash does in the sim, read from the
  game's own files: when each light comes on, in what colour, **per gear**, and how the lights blink at the shift point. The data
  comes with the plugin's car light database and updates on its own (no Lovely Car Data needed for these cars). On the wheel:
  - every gear has its own curve (the Porsche Cup cars, the GT3s, the prototypes...);
  - in USB mode the blink plays exactly as the car's dash plays it, frame by frame and in step with the sim's own clock;
  - in USB mode the **pit limiter lights** are the car's own, animated as on its dash, and change with your speed over the pit
    limit where the car's dash does (e.g. the BMW M4 GT3);
  - cars whose cockpit isn't installed on the PC the data was made on show their thresholds with the usual green, yellow and red.
- **Rev lights only**, per-gear cars: the rev lights now switch with the gear as they do on the FX Pro (the GT Neo's own
  per-gear mode in SimPro left them dark), and every colour is sent as one of SimPro's own (a lime green no longer shows as
  yellow).

Everything else is as in [0.7.5](https://github.com/fxunleashed/fx-unleashed/releases/tag/v0.7.5).

### Known issue

- **Rev lights only on the GT Neo:** with some cars the lights the car doesn't use can show blue once the first light comes on.
  We're looking into it; with "Rev lights only" off (the plugin drawing every light) it doesn't happen.

### Requirements

SimHub 9.11 or newer, SimPro Manager 3 (V3.2.2 tested) for standard mode and for "Rev lights only", an FX Pro, GT Neo or FX.
Tried by the maintainer on a GT Neo on an Alpha EVO base. Please report what you find (Discord or GitHub issues), especially a car
whose lights don't match its dash in the sim.

### Install and update

From 0.5.x, 0.6.x or 0.7.x: let it update itself (About tab). New install: close SimHub, copy `User.FXProRpmSync.dll` from the zip into your SimHub folder, start SimHub,
turn the plugin on. Guide: https://fxunleashed.com/start/
