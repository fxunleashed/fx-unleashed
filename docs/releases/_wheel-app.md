### Wheel app for Unleashed mode

Unleashed mode (your own dashes, all 38 lights, the dash button) needs our wheel app, build {BUILD} or newer. Standard mode needs nothing.

- **[Download the wheel app](https://github.com/fxunleashed/fx-unleashed-firmware/releases/latest)**
- **[Install guide](https://fxunleashed.com/start/#1-install-the-wheel-app)**: seven simple steps, about 10 minutes
- Please read the **[short notice](https://fxunleashed.com/firmware/#risks)** first: it is your wheel's own program, at your own risk, and Simagic's original is always one SimPro reinstall away.
