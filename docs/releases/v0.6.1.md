## FX Unleashed 0.6.1

Updates itself from 0.5.x and 0.6.0: the banner (or the About tab) offers it, one click installs it, **Roll back** is there if you want the old one.

### What's new

- **The LMP3 Ginetta G61 dash.** Converted 1:1 from [Redadeg's SimHub dash on lmu-dashboards.com](https://lmu-dashboards.com) and
  checked item by item against the original: fuel per lap and fuel level, speed, gear, best / last / predicted lap, brake temperatures,
  TC and TC cut (FPS), oil and water temperatures, the headlights, the pit limiter banner, the ignition-off screen and the ignition-on
  logo. The tyre panel has three pages, like the original: tyre pressures, brake temperatures (blue when cold, red when hot) and tyre
  temperatures; bind **Next / Previous page** to a wheel button. It comes with the plugin and is in the library.
- **SLIPSTREAM: "THIS LAP WON'T COUNT" in full.** The invalid-lap screen showed "THIS LAP WON'T": one command to the screen holds 58
  characters, and the label's position and colours took the rest. Fixed (SLIPSTREAM 1.0.1, also in the library).
- **Logos on wheels without the screen's RAM patch.** A picture with a transparent background (a logo) was drawn with a square of the
  wrong colour around it; it's now drawn on what's under it. Dashes with such pictures also draw a little less (the Mustang: about 18 %
  less when the dash first appears).
- **Checks for dash makers.** The designer's check and `fxdash check` now report a text the wheel would cut (too long for one screen
  command, even when it fits its box), with what to do about it.

Everything else is as in [0.6.0](https://github.com/fxunleashed/fx-unleashed/releases/tag/v0.6.0).

### Requirements

SimHub 9.11 or newer, SimPro Manager 3 (V3.2.2 tested) for standard mode, an FX Pro or GT Neo. Tested by the maintainer on one FX Pro on an Alpha EVO
base: other bases, wheels and games may behave differently, so please report what you find (Discord or GitHub issues).

### Install and update

From 0.5.x or 0.6.0: let it update itself (About tab). New install: close SimHub, copy `User.FXProRpmSync.dll` from the zip into your SimHub folder, start SimHub,
turn the plugin on. Guide: https://fxunleashed.com/start/
